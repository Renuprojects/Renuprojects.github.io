Projects & Case Studies

01 / Manufacturing P&L Analytics

10-Page Estimated vs Actual P&L Platform for a Packaging Manufacturer

Problem

The client — a print and packaging manufacturer serving US beauty brands — had no unified way to compare estimated vs actual revenue, cost, and margin across jobs, customers, and work centres. Downtime and make-ready data were tracked manually, with no trend visibility.

Solution

Built a 10-page Power BI report covering: Estimated vs Actual profit and margin by end market; Revenue trend with variance; Job-level drillthrough to work-centre cost breakdown; Customer and job profitability; Work Centre cost, scrap, and labor efficiency; MTD/PMTD Downtime and Make Ready with equipment-level detail; Invoice Margin and Domestic Outsource margin analysis by year.

Result

Delivered a single self-service platform replacing multiple disconnected reports. Business users can filter by date, customer, job code, work centre, and status — and drill from summary to individual job cost breakdown in two clicks.

Power BI DAX Drillthrough Manufacturing KPIs MTD/PMTD Cost Analytics
02 / Specialty Pharma Analytics

High-Impact Specialty Pharma Analytics Initiative

Problem

A specialty pharma client lacked a unified analytics layer to track Patient Journey, patient persistency, and market share. Without a structured data model, brand teams had no consistent view of critical performance metrics across the product lifecycle.

Solution

Spearheaded the end-to-end analytics initiative — built a robust star schema (6 dimensions + 3 facts) in SQL Server and delivered 8 dynamic Power BI pages with complex DAX measures including rolling 4-week, 12-week, and 52-week windows to unlock critical insights into Patient Journey, patient persistency, and market share.

Result

Gave the brand team a single, trusted analytics platform with real-time visibility into patient behaviour and market performance — replacing manual, disconnected reporting with standardised KPIs and time-intelligence DAX measures.

SQL Server Star Schema Power BI DAX Patient Journey Patient Persistency Rolling Windows Market Share
03 / Azure Medallion Pipeline

Built End-to-End Medallion Architecture for OTD and Labor Efficiency Reporting

Problem

Client needed reliable On-Time Delivery and Labor Efficiency reporting across multiple ERP systems with no consistent data pipeline in place.

Solution

Designed Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers in Azure Databricks ingesting from 5 source types including Radius ERP. Built ADF pipelines for automated ingestion, transformation, and Power BI refresh. Used dual date-logic DAX modelling for complex time intelligence.

Result

20+ production dashboards running on automated daily refresh. Eliminated manual reporting and gave business users self-service access to production insights without IT support.

04 / Production Tracking

Consolidated 4 Standalone Reports into a Single Production Tracker

Problem

Business owners were switching between 4 separate reports — actual, planned, demand, and forecast — with different refresh timelines, making it difficult to get a single consistent view of production status.

Solution

Merged all four data sources into a single SQL stored procedure handling daily, weekly, and monthly refresh logic. Built one Power BI report with a unified view and decommissioned the four standalone reports.

Result

Eliminated report-switching overhead for business owners. The consolidated tracker became the single source of truth for production tracking across the team.
